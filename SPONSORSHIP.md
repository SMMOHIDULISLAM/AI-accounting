# Support AI Accounting

The goal is accessible Bangla and English bookkeeping for small businesses, with reviewed messaging input and an expandable manufacturing workflow. The local learning preview runs without a paid AI API. Development, hosting and independent professional review still need support.

We welcome volunteer accounting review, accessibility/Bangla testing, security and backup review, documentation, and carefully scoped development contributions. Funding priorities are reliable recovery, private company trials, real channel integrations and jurisdiction-specific controls. See [current scope](docs/PLATFORM-SETUP.md) and the [professional review package](docs/PROFESSIONAL-REVIEW.md).

To discuss sponsorship, [open a project issue](https://github.com/SMMOHIDULISLAM/AI-accounting/issues/new) with your proposed scope. Do not include financial records, payment credentials or private contact details. There is currently **no configured donation checkout, payout account or promise of investment returns**. The repository's funding button links to this information page.

GitHub Sponsors eligibility depends on the recipient's supported region. Bangladesh was not on the official recipient list when checked on 27 November 2026. Confirm eligibility or join the official waitlist before setting up a recipient account; never misstate residency. [GitHub Sponsors documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors/getting-started-with-github-sponsors/about-github-sponsors).

An optional static sponsor card can be enabled by the deploy owner with public build values `VITE_SPONSOR_URL` (HTTPS) and `VITE_SPONSOR_NAME`. It is off by default, labelled Sponsored, and loads no advertising SDK or tracking script. This is not an activated advertising network, ad approval or verified income stream. Sponsorship must not influence accounting results or imply professional endorsement.

Third-party code retains its own licensing. A repository-wide first-party redistribution license has not yet been established; public visibility and a funding link do not by themselves grant permission to relicense inherited code. Resolve ownership and licensing before representing the entire repository as a permissively licensed product.
